# The Dither Decides

Published: 2025-03-09
Tag: LOG

Between two colours there is no third. When a gradient has to survive on this screen it is handed to the dither, and the dither does not blend; it votes. Every pixel stands up and declares itself ink or paper.

This is why the dithered image feels more honest than the smooth one. The smooth gradient hides a thousand tiny rounding errors and calls them taste. The dither shows its arithmetic. Zoom in and the pattern confesses: here I ran out of resolution, here I chose.

Interfaces could learn from that. A state that cannot afford to be vague should not be allowed to blur. Quantize the decision, show the dots, let the pattern be the proof.
