# The Cursor Blinks First

Published: 2023-12-02
Tag: LOG

Before any window opens, before the grid fades in, the cursor is already there: one block of light, pulsing at a frequency nobody configured. It is the only element on this machine that breathes before it is asked to.

The blink is a clock with nothing to count. It divides the night into identical halves — on, off, on, off — and waits for someone to give the rhythm a meaning. Typing does that. Every character lands on the beat and pushes it one step forward.

That is the whole exchange, really. The machine offers a pulse; the operator offers a reason. Everything else on this desktop is decoration around that handshake.
