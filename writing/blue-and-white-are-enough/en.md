# Blue and White Are Enough

Published: 2025-07-12
Tag: ESSAY

A palette of two colours is not a limitation; it is a compression algorithm. Every state the interface can be in has to survive being described as ink or paper, so nothing vague makes it to the screen.

Selected is paper on ink. Disabled is ink at half confidence. Danger is not red; danger is a clearer sentence. The constraint removes decoration and leaves decisions.

Two colours are enough. Most interfaces would also be fine with one font, one grid, and one honest shadow.
