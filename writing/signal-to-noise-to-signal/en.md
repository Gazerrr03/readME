# Signal to Noise to Signal

Published: 2024-03-16
Tag: NOTES

Noise has a bad reputation it never earned. Static is just a signal from a source too large to name: the whole sky, transmitting at once, on every frequency you can afford to hear.

The broadcasters of two in the morning know this. Between the last ident and the test tone there is a stretch of carrier that carries nothing, and that nothing is the most honest thing on the dial. No agenda, no caller, no request.

A portfolio could aspire to that much honesty. Not the pitch, not the reel — the carrier. Proof that someone is at the station, keeping the transmitter warm, whether or not anyone is tuned in.
